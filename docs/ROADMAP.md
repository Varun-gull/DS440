# Roadmap

## Working Now

- Authentication and profiles
- Resume text extraction and keyword identification
- Internship and new-graduate posting discovery
- Rule-based fit scoring
- Application tracking and recruiting calendar
- XP, points, streaks, ranks, badges, challenges, and rewards
- Leaderboards, friends, groups, public profiles, and messaging
- STAR interview answer storage

## Capstone Priority 1: Establish the Baseline

- Freeze and document the current scoring formula.
- Create a reproducible resume/job-description evaluation set.
- Collect human relevance and skill-match labels.
- Measure keyword extraction and fit-score quality.

## Capstone Priority 2: Add Genuine AI

- Add server-side structured resume and job-description extraction.
- Add semantic resume-to-job matching.
- Produce evidence-grounded match explanations.
- Identify matched, missing, and uncertain qualifications.
- Generate truthful, editable resume suggestions.
- Log model name, version, settings, latency, and cost.

## Capstone Priority 3: Evaluate

- Compare baseline and AI outputs on identical examples.
- Measure agreement with human reviewers.
- Measure unsupported recommendation and hallucination rates.
- Collect usefulness and clarity ratings from students.
- Test privacy, prompt injection, bias, and failure cases.

## Later Enhancements

- AI-assisted interview feedback
- Personalized weekly career plans
- Recommendation feedback controls
- Better analytics and outcome tracking
- Approved live-job API integration
- Accessibility and mobile audits

## Out of Scope

- Automatically applying on a user’s behalf
- Scraping restricted job platforms
- Inventing resume qualifications
- Making employer hiring decisions
- Training a large language model from scratch
- Treating a fit score as the probability of receiving an offer
